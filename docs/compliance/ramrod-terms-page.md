# RAMROD Terms

RAMROD is a workflow tool for inventory intake, price research, marketplace listing preparation and fulfillment coordination.

## Scope

RAMROD supports the preparation of resale listings for collectibles, toys, games, trading cards and related inventory.

## Human Review

AI-generated identifications, prices, descriptions and marketplace routes are suggestions. Users are responsible for reviewing item identity, condition, price, legal sale eligibility and marketplace policy compliance before publishing listings.

## Marketplace Publishing

RAMROD may create draft listing data and, where authorized, interact with marketplace APIs. Publishing to third-party marketplaces should only occur after explicit user approval.

## Pricing

Price estimates are based on available signals and may be inaccurate. RAMROD does not guarantee sale price, sell-through rate or marketplace demand.

## Prohibited Items

Users must not use RAMROD to list unlawful, restricted, counterfeit or marketplace-prohibited goods.
